## About the Project

**MindFlow** is a mental wellness app offering guided meditations, breathing exercises, and procedurally generated natural audio environments for focus and deep sleep.

---

## Key Features

- **Spatial Audio Engine**: Infinite ambient soundscape generation.
- **Mood Tracking Journal**: Quick emotional check-ins with AI trend analysis.
- **Haptic Breathing Guides**: Tactile feedback assisting breathing pacing.
- **Sleep Integration**: Smart fade-out timer synced with Apple Health sleep data.

### Results

- **Best Design Nominee** in Health & Fitness categories.
- Average user session duration increased to **24 minutes**.
