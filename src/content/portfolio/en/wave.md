## About the Project

**Wave** is an audio-first social platform centered around live voice rooms and short audio notes with automated speech-to-text transcription across 30+ languages.

---

## Key Features

- **Live WebRTC Audio Rooms**: Ultra-low latency (<100ms) supporting hundreds of live listeners.
- **AI Audio Transcription**: Instant text captions for voice notes.
- **AI Noise Cancellation**: Crystal-clear sound filtering out background noise.
- **Voice Effects & Filters**: Real-time voice equalizer and audio presets.

---

### Results

- Over **1,200,000 minutes** of voice conversations daily.
- **35%** reduction in cellular data consumption using the Opus codec.
