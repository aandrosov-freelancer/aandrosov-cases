## About the Project

**Wanderly** is your smart travel companion. It combines offline vector maps, curated local insider guides, and collaborative itinerary planning in real time.

---

## Key Features

- **Offline 3D Vector Maps**: Full navigation without roaming data.
- **Collaborative Trip Planning**: Edit itineraries with friends live.
- **Smart Ticket Organizer**: Automatic flight and hotel reservation import.
- **Audio Walking Tours**: Exclusive local guide audio stories.

---

### Results

- Offline maps saved users **over 10 TB** of roaming data.
- Top-10 app in the Travel category in 14 countries.
