## About the Project

**FitTrack** is a modern fitness application engineered for automated workout tracking, heart rate monitoring, and tailored physical activity plans.

The primary goal of the project was to make maintaining a healthy lifestyle intuitive, engaging, and backed by accurate biometric analytics.

---

## Key Features

- **HealthKit & Wearables Integration**: Seamless real-time sync with smartwatches and fitness bands.
- **Adaptive Workout Tracking**: Smart workload recommendations tailored to the user's current fatigue and recovery level.
- **Interactive Analytics**: Rich charts for progress monitoring, calorie expenditure, and sleep quality.
- **Offline Mode**: Full workout recording capability without active cellular or Wi-Fi connectivity.

---

## Technical Highlights

Built using **Clean Architecture + MVVM-C**, ensuring robust testability and modularity across features.
